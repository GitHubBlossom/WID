Subject: Custom Pattern Regex Help

Description:
We're setting up custom patterns for our internal API tokens. The format is "TST_" followed by 32 hex characters. What regex should we use?

---
Support Response (2025-01-06, 30 minutes later):
Hi Priya! The pattern would be: TST_[a-fA-F0-9]{32}

You can add it under Settings > Custom Patterns with severity "High" since these are production tokens.
